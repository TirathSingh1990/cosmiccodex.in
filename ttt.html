How to play
1. Hold your phone flat. Motion sensing starts automatically when the page opens.
2. Tilt your phone gently to roll the ball. Tap Calibrate level whenever the board is resting flat in your hands.
3. Reach the glowing golden hole. Any dark hole or the board edge is a fall — game over.
Tip: Play in portrait orientation. Motion sensors generally require HTTPS (or localhost). Sound starts after your first tap.
